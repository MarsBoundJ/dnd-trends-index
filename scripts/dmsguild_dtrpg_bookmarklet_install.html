⚔ DMsGuild / DTRPG Incursion

The panel header is the version test. Click the bookmarklet on metal.php and read the top of the panel.

Current build says ⚔ INCURSION V11 and lists Shelves on page: Adamantine, Mithral, Platinum followed by a count per tier.

Old build says ⚔ INCURSION V9 and shows only a total. If you see V9, the install did not take.

That breakdown cannot be produced by the old build, which is what makes it a test rather than a reassurance.
Why this version: the tier field was wrong from day one. V9 decided a product's metal tier by walking backwards and asking “does any preceding element mention a metal?” — and product titles mention metals. Measured on the live page Sep 16, 2026: “Trophy Gold” made the next product Gold, “A Copper For A Song Battlemaps” made it Copper, “B3 Palace of the Silver Princess” made it Silver.

metal.php has three shelves — Adamantine, Mithral, Platinum — yet about 40% of every capture came back Gold, Silver or Copper: tiers with no section on the page at all. V11 matches the section heading exactly and anchored, which a product title cannot satisfy, and reports Unknown rather than guessing when a product sits under no heading.

It also sends product_url, which was NULL on every row ever shipped, leaving these products joinable only by title string.

Where to run it

These two pages, and no others. They are the only place either site exposes the sales medals:

  • https://www.dmsguild.com/metal.php — expect roughly 1,100 products
  • https://www.drivethrurpg.com/metal.php — expect roughly 1,360

The bookmarklet cannot read the modern /en/browse pages at all — measured Sep 16: 0 of 60 products captured, because the new Angular site uses different markup. Only the legacy metal.php layout works.

Before you transmit

  1. Wait out Cloudflare. Both sites show a “Just a moment…” interstitial first.
  2. Scroll to the bottom so every shelf renders. A short run on May 19 caught Gold at 42 against a usual ~437 because the lower shelves had not loaded.
  3. Check the total against the figures above before pressing Transmit.
  4. Transmit once per page, per day. The ingest route has no same-day dedup — unlike the Amazon ranks route, which refuses a second batch. On Apr 19 a second transmit doubled that day's rows in every tier.
What the tiers mean. Adamantine is the highest medal, not Platinum — DriveThruRPG's own legend lists the levels in ascending order Copper → Silver → Electrum → Gold → Platinum → Mithral → Adamantine, with Adamantine at 0.2% of the catalogue and Copper at 12.34%. The rarest tiers are the ones worth having, and they are the two this page captures most completely.
